- `pytest` of #34 failed on three cobertura tests from `tests/test_gate_coverage.py` that #29 brought in.

  It lacks `tests/fixtures/gates/coverage-cobertura/gate_out/coverage.xml`, because the `coverage.xml` rule in `.gitignore` hid the file.
- The Gates of #29 passed, because the Probe runs in the Workspace without `clean: true` and sees the untracked file.
  Ticket #29 merged with red tests, and #34 paid for it.
- The `fix` agent found the cause in 58 s, restored the fixture and lifted the rule for `tests/fixtures/gates/**/coverage.xml`.
  The second `pytest` Probe passed.

## What the Brief was missing

- The Brief carries the Spec, the Ticket and `cause`, but not the ADRs.
  Ticket #29 spoke of "the threshold from `with`", and the agent made a single `threshold`.
  The agent did not read ADR 0005 with `min_changed` and `min_total`.
- Ticket #29 spoke of lines changed "relative to the Ticket's `base`".
  The agent did exactly that and noted itself that after `sync` these lines include lines of other Tickets.
  The review of PR #74 did not fix this point, and `min_changed` still judges `ticket.base..HEAD`.
- The Roles' prompts do not say how to run checks.
  The implementers found `uv run` on their own, but the `resolve` agent did not (#76).

## What the engine is missing

- Gates should judge the tree that goes to `merge`, but now they also see the Workspace's ignored files (#75).
- After `sync`, `min_changed` should compare against the merged tip, not against `ticket.base`.
  There is no issue for this yet.
- The `reason` of `OperationFinished` for a `sync` conflict ends with the empty output of `git merge-tree`.
  The list of files is only in `conflict-N.json`.
- The conflict state and the Probe results for a Gate with `from` live only in the Orchestrator's memory.
  #38 is to restore them from the Journal.
- Pipeline `self` has no reviewer and no Decision `risk`.
  The bugs the owner's review found could have been found by a review Stage, but the Decision after it waits for the human Decider from #35.
